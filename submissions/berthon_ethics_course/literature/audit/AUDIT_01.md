Method. For each facet I took the quotations, numbers, dates, locators and attributions in section (c) and the entries in (b) they rest on, and searched the saved source text (`~/iit-playground/berthon_ethics_local/pdfs/A` to `D`) with `pdftotext`/Python text matching, normalising whitespace, hyphenation and the long s. Where the saved text was missing or empty I re-extracted it (Alabama 1832 acts: my own tesseract OCR of the PDF; Waitangi report: per-page `pdftotext` because the saved two-column text interleaves; Clanchy: live Google Books search-inside snippets, the same route the facet used). "CONFIRMED" means I saw the words, number or claim in the source text this session. OCR damage is noted when it limits the match. An "item" is one quotation, number, date, locator or attribution; a row can hold several items, and the verdict column gives the count.
