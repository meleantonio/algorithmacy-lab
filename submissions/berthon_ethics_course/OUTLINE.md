## (b) Section plan

**§1. The question.** A competence becomes a condition of ordinary standing. Who gains a position over
the people who must acquire it, and what did they do with it last time?
- Draws on: Vallor (2024, p. 196) on protection against "new and better ends"
  (`sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:14`); Jobin et al., 60 of 84 guidelines against 41 (`:22`).
- Terms: algorithmacy (`:97`); the literacy transition and "ethical failure" (`RESEARCH_PLAN.md` §2).

**§2. The standard.** What licenses calling a past practice a failure.
- Sources: facet X, Q2 (Hunt; Butterfield; Levy's split between condemning a practice and blaming
  persons). Socrates on writing (`sources/part2_ethics_DRAFT.md:71`) as a contemporary objecting to
  the transition itself.
- Carries the qualification at `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:85`: no cognitive claim; and the
  counter-evidence of P9, that people sought literacy and it delivered.

**§3. Five things done with literacy.** One subsection each; each gives what was done, to whom, on
what stated ground, who objected, what ended it.
1. *The gate.* Facet D, F1–F3. The framers' statements; registrar discretion; *Gaston*; Wilson's veto.
2. *The withheld competence.* Facet C, Failures 1–2. The statutes at their actual scope; Auld; the
   Douglasses; More's "no writing for the poor".
3. *The record over the voice.* Facet A, Failures 1–3. *Marshall* ¶14, ¶40; *Delgamuukw* ¶98; Waitangi;
   Barnet.
4. *The monopoly.* Facet B, Failure 1. The charter; the 1662 Act; Milton.
5. *The promise.* Facet F, Q1, Q3, Q5. Jomtien and Dakar; the 2024 count; Freire.
- One paragraph on why imposed schooling is not a sixth (facet E, "Claims the sources contradict" 1).
- Do not use: Innis as a wrong done to persons; print as the cause of dialect loss; any count of people
  excluded in the print era; Graff's require/reward distinction as his.

**§4. The same positions, occupied again.** The comparison table of `T_adjudication.md` §4, one
paragraph per row, each naming the property compared.
- The gate: `sources/part2_ethics_DRAFT.md:25` (shared screening vendors; credit invisibles);
  `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:118` (dialect penalties).
- The withheld competence: `sources/part2_ethics_DRAFT.md:25, 83` (the fifteen-second offer; Rahman;
  Beane).
- The record: `sources/part2_ethics_DRAFT.md:29` (template replies; appeals); `:23` (Hirschman on voice).
- The monopoly: `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:116`, which needs a source before it is used.
- The promise: `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:93` (reading the black box); facet Z, Q2 (AI-literacy
  rules); `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:120` (Berthon and Pitt; decision 6).
- State for each row where the parallel is weak (the table's last column).

**§5. Where the comparison stops.** Adaptivity and binding outcomes (facet X, Q3; P13).
- The rebuilt table replaces `sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:101–106`.
- The history's standing, per decision 1.

**§6. What the earlier failures ask of this transition.** The obligation at
`sources/part2_ethics_DRAFT.md:101` and the three design principles at
`sources/design_ethics_DRAFT.md:128`, each set against the failure it answers: structural refusal
against the gate, a declared operating envelope and conditions for learning against withholding,
counter-delegation against the one-sided record.
- This pairing is Claude's proposal and needs the author's ruling before it is used.
