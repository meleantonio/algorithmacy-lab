# AUDIT_01: citation audit of the section (c) syntheses in facets A, B and C

**Date:** 2026-10-12. **Auditor:** independent (did not write the facets). **Nothing in the facet files or elsewhere has been changed.**

**Scope.** `## (c) Synthesis` (through to `## (d)`) in `literature/facets/A_the_record.md`, `B_the_dispute.md`, `C_spacing_to_literacy.md`. Section (b) read only to see what each agent says it read.

**Checks run.**
1. Local texts in `dxd_foundation_local/pdfs/A` and `/B` (Knox, Heilmann, O'Donnell, Garrett, the reviews of Johnson 2010; Saenger OCR). Page locators for Knox and Heilmann recomputed from running heads.
2. Saenger, independent route 1: Google Books search-inside (ID `w3vZaFoaa3EC`), which returns the printed page number. Route 2: archive.org full-text search (`be-api.us.archive.org/fts/v1/search`) restricted to the lending scan `spacebetweenword0000saen`, which returns the wording but no page. The archive.org "inside.php" route is refused to guests.
3. Other archive.org scans through the same full-text search: Johnson 2000 (AJP 121.4), Vatri 2012 (CQ 62.2), Zanker 1991, Wood 1929, Parkes 1992, Cressy 1980, Scribner and Cole 1981, Clanchy 1993, Harris 1989, Bowman and Thomas, Tab. Vindol. II.
4. Crossref record for every DOI in the three `.bib` files (43 DOIs): title, container, volume, pages, year, authors. Crossref `filter=updates:<DOI>` for every one.
5. Each quotation read against the claim hung on it.

**Result in one line.** The quotations are mostly accurate (Saenger, Knox, Heilmann, O'Donnell, Johnson, Vatri all check). The errors are in what the syntheses build on them: a "1040s" date and a "later eleventh" date that Saenger's text contradicts, a claim that Saenger does not link spacing to literacy diffusion that p. 11 qualifies, a mis-correction of facet E on p. 69, a wrong chapter number, and several uses of reviewers' words as if they were the author's. Crossref shows no retraction or correction notice on any DOI.

**Saenger independent-confirmation count.** 25 distinct Saenger quotations or dated claims appear in the three syntheses. I confirmed wording on at least one independent route for 24: wording and printed page on both routes for 19; wording on the lending scan only (page not independently confirmed) for 5 (p. 7 "Psychologists concede", p. 17 "intimately linked" (second half only, via Google), p. 243 "became the norm", p. 266 "1500", pp. 264-265 "emboldened"). One (p. 85 "shortcuts") is confirmed by Google for the phrase "prolonged and arduous" only. Every quotation not confirmed on both routes carries NEEDS LIBRARY CHECK for its page. The local OCR text was used only to test whether a quoted string and its page number agree internally; where a row says "local OCR" that is all it means. The local OCR page markers fail after about p. 176 (they collapse to "185"), so locators beyond that rest on A's reading plus the Google pages I report.

Status key: VERIFIED verbatim / VERIFIED paraphrase / WRONG / OVERSTATED / NEEDS LIBRARY CHECK / COULD NOT CHECK.
