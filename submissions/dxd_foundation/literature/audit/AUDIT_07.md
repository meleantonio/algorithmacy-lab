## Is "the working disclosures work by re-representation, not by reading" the sources' analysis or the facet agent's?

**Label: the facet agent's inference.** No source uses "re-representation", and none says the successful disclosures work "not by reading".

- What the sources do say. Ben-Shahar and Schneider (p. 743): "length, complexity, and difficulty are the enemies of successful mandates ... brief, simple, easy disclosures are at least preferable ... perhaps to the point of using symbols instead of sentences ... This is information that anyone can understand." Fung et al. (p. 57): both Los Angeles grades and rollover ratings "convert data and expert interpretation into simple normative signals such as stars or letter grades", and a rating system is "one way to simplify the format of complex information"; (p. 55) people use window-front grades because "the added cost of obtaining new information ... is very low". That supports a weaker claim: simple, cheap, symbolic signals are what succeed. It is a claim about simplicity and cost, and a letter grade is still something a customer reads.
- What they say against "not by reading". (i) Ben-Shahar and Schneider hedge the example ("There is little real evidence, but we find some reason to think", p. 743) and state on pp. 744 and 748 that grade cards also work because disclosers "try to get their grades up", so "disclosees need not rely on the disclosed data". Their own footnote (p. 743, n. 420) cites Jin and Leslie: the effect on hospital admissions "is not fully explained by consumers switching". (ii) Loewenstein et al. (p. 403, n. 8) call the restaurant case "a case often cited as a paradigm for successful disclosure" but add that "to some extent including the case of restaurant hygiene ratings, an industry response can be found amid little evidence of a consumer response", and that grade cards "led inspectors to distort their ratings". (iii) Fung et al. make success depend on "double embeddedness", the discloser's response as much as the user's (p. 65).
- So the part the sources support is "simple signals"; "re-representation" is the facet's frame (the author's type (i) move); "not by reading" is unsupported, and for the restaurant grades the sources point to a discloser-side mechanism instead. The facet edits now label it "my inference".
