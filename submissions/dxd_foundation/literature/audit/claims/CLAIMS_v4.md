## Cross-cutting findings (read these first)

1. **Reference list and citations are complete.** All 135 reference entries are cited in the body and all in-text citations have an entry; every entry has a card. No orphan either way.
2. **Pagination is the main systematic defect.** About 35 sources were read in a preprint, working-paper, author-manuscript or advance-access version, and the body cites those versions' page numbers while the reference list gives the journal's. Any reader checking the journal will not find the pages. Affected (version read → journal pagination): Shneiderman 2020 (arXiv pp. 1–21 → 495–504); Lai et al. 2023 (arXiv 2021 → 1369–1385; title and author order differ); Wiener & Curry 1980 (NASA TM → 995–1011); Rochet & Tirole 2006 (IDEI WP → 645–667); Hagiu & Wright 2015 (HBS WP → 162–174); Castillo et al. 2025 (2017 draft, different title → 4377–4395); Garg & Nazerzadeh 2022 (arXiv → 3219–3235); Chen, Mislove & Wilson 2016 (pdf pp. 2, 7, 10 → 1340, 1345, 1348); Farronato et al. 2023 (NBER WP → 239–243); Haldar et al. 2019 (arXiv → 1927–1935); Vallas & Schor 2020 ("16.10", advance → 273–294); Hancock et al. 2020 (advance pp. 1–12 → 89–100; add 88); Hardt et al. 2016 (arXiv → 111–122); Kleinberg & Raghavan 2020 (arXiv → 1–23); Danz et al. 2022 (NBER WP → 2851–2883); Wang et al. 2023 (arXiv → 2297–2317); Luca & Zervas 2016 (accepted MS → 3412–3427); Bishop 2019, Curchod et al. 2020, Hassidim et al. 2021, Shorrer & Sóvágó 2023, Burton-Chellew & West 2021 (author MSS); Risko & Gilbert 2016 (ms. pp. 2, 33 → 676–688); Gilbert et al. 2020 (PsyArXiv → 501–517); Kayongo et al. 2022 (author version → 465–474); Hollan et al. 2000 (ms. → 174–196); Li 2017 (MPRA WP → 3257–3287); Li 2024 (arXiv → 175–192); Martínez-Marquina et al. 2019 (draft → 3437–3474); Breitmoser & Schweighofer-Kodritsch 2022 (DP 151 → 483–513); Bó & Hakimov 2024 (arXiv → 4693–4721); Rees-Jones & Skowronek 2018 (arXiv → 11471–11476); Casas-Arce et al. 2017 (WP → 1051–1088); Hogarth & Soyer 2011 (UPF WP, different title → 434–463); McKay et al. 2022 (preprint → art. 102165); Chou et al. 2009 (Caltech WP → 159–179); Matsuo et al. 2026 (arXiv → 650–661); Gonczarowski et al. 2024 (NBER WP; fine as cited). §9 names only four of these. Either cite the versions read in the reference list, or convert every locator before submission.
3. **Two contradictions,** both in the draft's own framing sentences rather than in cited numbers: line 17 ("the letters were the same") against Tunbridge pp. 165–175 and the draft's own §6.1; and line 192 ("in each setting the convention moved toward the weaker party under outside force and not on the third party's initiative") against the paragraph's own cases (Google and Uber changed their rules on their own initiative).
4. **Four miscitations:** one non-verbatim quotation (Onnasch et al., line 37), one quotation given the wrong page (Cofone & Strandburg, line 92), one set of figures given the wrong pages (Luca & Zervas, line 98), and one experimental result attached to the wrong group (Hogarth & Soyer, line 162: the 17%/97% belong to the sophisticated group, not the naive adults).
5. **Bib-field discrepancies** (also noted in the draft's own notes): Ghalme et al. is listed as an arXiv preprint but appeared in PMLR 139:3672–3681 (ICML 2021); Stark & Pais carries a 2020 volume date but was published 29 January 2021 (OpenAlex: 2021); Heiland is dated 2025 in the list (volume year) and 2023 on the card (accepted 5 Nov 2023); Bucher et al. is 2020 in the list (online) and 28(1) 2021 in print; Castillo et al. 2025 and Hogarth & Soyer 2011 were read under different titles from the ones listed.
