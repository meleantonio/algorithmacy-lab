### Upheld (13)

- **C04-01** (needs_citation). The sentence is uncited, and the next citation (line 162) attaches to a different claim. B&S p. 647 would carry the legal half. Nothing in the library covers "design standards".
- **C04-02** (overstated). Aloisi & Potocka-Sionek: "The introduction of mere information rights would not be enough" (p. 39, re-read). "Universal" fails.
- **C04-09** (overstated). The advice came after the instructions were read aloud and a 17-question quiz was answered and marked (p. 908). Masuda et al. themselves say the advice works "by promoting a better understanding of the strategy-proofness" (abstract, p. 902; p. 904). Result 4 (p. 917) stands at 15.0% [10.1%, 20.0%]. One caveat cuts against the ledger: in the random-effects logit, Advice × Perfect Score is "insignificant" (p. 917). The quiz–advice link is therefore not robust, but the authors' own reading is still understanding, not "nothing to study".
