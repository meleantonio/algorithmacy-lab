## Auditor caveats

- **Springer, Sage, INFORMS, Elsevier and SSRN returned bot walls or HTTP 403** for several sources; I did not get round them. The local "PDFs" for Bar-Gill, Breitmoser, Sailer, Sanguinetti and Lurie in `pdfs/N/` are bot-wall HTML pages, not papers. Where I give an abstract, the row says where it came from.
- **Schroeder and Cenkci 2018** (58 comparisons, n = 2,426, g = 0.63): no abstract in Crossref, OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar; Springer blocked. UNCHECKABLE. A second source (Frontiers 2019) cites Ginns 2006 at d = 0.85 and does not give the Schroeder figure.
- **Jessoe and Rapson** full text not obtained (the Haas working paper link redirected to an empty eScholarship page). I could confirm only what the AER abstract says.
- **Guillén and Hakimov 2018 and Lurie and Swaminathan 2009**: abstracts only, from secondary reproductions. The Elsevier pages are walled.
- **The facet's absence claims** ("no controlled test of upfront fares", "no study in this set involved two humans and an adaptive third party") are claims about the facet's own search; I did not repeat the search.
- The "pre-registered" label on Gonczarowski et al. and Hakimov and Khanna is the authors' own (aspredicted 7eq7e; AEARCTR-0013135); I did not open the registries.
