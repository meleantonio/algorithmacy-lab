## Auditor caveats

- The word-separation question: none of Charlemagne's letter, *Admonitio generalis* c. 72, Alcuin carm. XCIV or Westwell mentions word separation. The facet keeps "correction" and "word spacing" apart correctly, but "for reading aloud" is supported only by Alcuin's "lector in ecclesia", and the facet's blanket "no source I opened links it to word separation" ignored Piazzoni's "well-spaced words" (a description of a script, not an aim of the reform) and Parkes's abstract (credits Irish and Anglo-Saxon scribes with the "grammar of legibility").
- The Latin texts are OCR of the MGH volumes; I read the lines in the facet and found them as quoted (c/e confusions aside). The letter is read in Munro's 1900 translation only; I did not check the Latin.
- Consensus was the source for the Parkes, Ganz, Mostert, Khan and Pozen and Stadelmann abstracts (Crossref and OpenAlex hold none, or the publisher elided it). Quotations from those were found in the Consensus text.
- Not opened: McKitterick, Ganz 1990, Bischoff, Ostrom 1990, Steinová, Dumville 1992, the Hilfr and Just Eat agreements, the Just Eat WageIndicator page, Help Net Security 2023, Doellgast 2022 full text, Neumann thesis PDF (embargoed until 1 June 2029), Mostert page range (112-127 from a catalogue snippet; Crossref has none), Delacroix volume pages.
- EUR-Lex was not needed; the Directive, DSA and EAA texts are the Official Journal XML/XHTML already downloaded to the local folder.
- Whether any Member State has already transposed Directive 2024/2831 was not checked.
