# Sources the lab already holds that bear on the foundation paper

*Built by Claude on 2026-10-12 from three read-only surveys of the repo. None of this is author text. I
listed what the earlier arms recorded; I did not re-read the sources.*

**How to use this file.** Each row is a lead. A facet agent reopens the source and records its own read
status before relying on it, because the recorded depths come from earlier runs. Paths are relative to
`submissions/`.

**Known hazards.**
- Saenger (1997) and Parkes (1992) were read only as forty-word Google Books snippets. Every quotation
  from them in Part 2 needs its context checked.
- Gavrilov (1997) is abstract-only and Burnyeat (1997) unread; "closed the question for classicists" is
  Johnson's characterisation (2000, p. 593), read from his opening paragraph only.
- The four Gemini essays (three in `dial_response_algorithmacy/sources/`, one at
  `triadic_reduction/sources/G7_tablets_to_algorithms.md`) are leads only. See `RESEARCH_PLAN.md` §6.
- `dial_response_algorithmacy/draft.md:337` attributes a point about *scriptio continua* to Inman and
  Ribes (2019), whose paper is about seamful design. Check before reuse.
- The published Part 1 says writing "rewired the neurological architecture of the human mind" (line
  120) when describing Ong and Goody and Watt. No audit covers that wording.
