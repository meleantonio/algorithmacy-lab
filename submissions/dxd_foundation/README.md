# The foundation paper for Decision Experience Design

**Status (2026-10-12):** researched, audited and outlined; no draft. The author writes the paper.
Claude wrote every file in this arm, and none of the wording is the author's unless quoted.

**Where the research landed.** The thesis as first given does not survive in three places, and a
narrower one does. Word separation lowered a cost of reading, most for readers still learning; no source
credits it with spreading literacy. It came as one of a complex of conventions. Nadella's post shares the
premise and the mechanism of external controls and differs on who holds authority and whom the design
serves. [`literature/facets/T_adjudication.md`](literature/facets/T_adjudication.md) gives the verdicts
and ten decisions for the author; [`OUTLINE.md`](OUTLINE.md) carries them into a section plan.

A paper for PHD1750-3 / HF700-1, Foundations in Human Factors (Bentley, Fall 2026). It replaces the DIAL
response ([`../dial_response_algorithmacy/`](../dial_response_algorithmacy/)) as the course paper and is
meant to found the Decision Experience Design series
([`../decision_experience_design/`](../decision_experience_design/)).

**The argument under test.** Scribes moved from *scriptio continua* to spaces between words and so took
the work of finding words off the reader. Algorithmacy needs a move of the same kind. Satya Nadella's
post of 10 October 2026, "Models as Insider Risks in the Super Intelligence Era", proposes the opposite:
observation and containment by the organisation. See [`CLAIM.md`](CLAIM.md).
